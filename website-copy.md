**H1:** Services
Independent cost advice for homes and businesses. We read the paperwork, explain it clearly and charge a flat fee.

### For individuals
Homeowners and renovators.
1. **Pre-construction quote audit:** We go through your contractor’s quote line by line and check scope, materials and rates. *You receive: a PDF report, in 3 working days*
2. **Residential design and material budgeting:** We turn your plan and material choices into an itemised budget, room by room. *You receive: an itemised budget and material list, in 5 working days*
3. **Milestone and execution tracking:** We check each payment stage against the work done before you pay. *You receive: a milestone checklist and a photo update at each stage*
- Button: Book for my home

### For businesses
Commercial spaces and developers.
1. **Commercial fit-out quote review:** We compare vendor quotes against your scope, side by side. *You receive: a side-by-side comparison report, in 5 working days*
2. **CAPEX and project budgeting advisory:** We help you set a budget your finance team can follow, and track it. *You receive: a CAPEX plan and a monthly report*
3. **Vendor and contract oversight:** We read contracts and watch delivery against scope, payments and timelines. *You receive: contract notes and a delivery tracker*
- Button: Book for my business

### For interior designers
Design assistance when you want an extra pair of hands.
1. **Room sketches and plans:** Layouts and room concepts, drafted to your brief and ready to refine with your client. *You receive: a plan and one concept per room, in [x working days]*
2. **Costed bills of quantities:** We turn your client’s plan into an itemised budget by material tier. *You receive: a costed bill of quantities and a review call*
3. **Quote audits and vendor support:** We review vendor quotes line by line and shortlist vendors from our checked panel. *You receive: a plain-language report and a vendor shortlist*
- Button: See monthly plans

### A note on fairness
We respect every kind of provider, from turnkey firms to independent carpenters. Our job is to make a quote clear, not to take sides against anyone.

## FAQ (shown on Home, How it works and Pricing)

**Do you build or sell interiors?**
No. We don’t build, supply or sell anything except advice, and we take no commission from contractors or suppliers.

**Will you tell me whether a quote is fair?**
We tell you what we find: what is clear, what is missing and what is worth asking about. We don’t guarantee savings, and the final decision is always yours.

**Can I keep my contractor?**
Yes. Many clients ask us to review the quote from the contractor they already like. We review the quote as it is and never ask you to change contractor.

**Can I pay per service or monthly?**
Yes. Every service can be bought one at a time at the price shown. Monthly plans are for interior designers and businesses with regular work, and extra requests in a plan are billed at the pay-per-request price.

**Is there any other cost?**
No percentage of your project cost and no commission from anyone. Any site visit you ask for is listed in your fixed estimate.
